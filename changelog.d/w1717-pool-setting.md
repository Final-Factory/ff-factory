- **An operator's Claude billing can go to another operator's pool** (w1717): Settings → Claude pool, one row, "Bill my work to": "My own pool" (the default, as before) or another operator's pool, set by each operator for themselves; a person's orchestrator can with `set_pool`. It moves all of their Claude billing: their workers and standing agents (new requests and the ones they act on), their orchestrator, the ops worker's jobs for them, and the unpinned dispatcher with the system payer's setting; never their GitHub token. The picked pool keeps its own caps, reserve and stop line, and workers wait while it is held (docs/vault.md section 4, "Picking another person's pool").
