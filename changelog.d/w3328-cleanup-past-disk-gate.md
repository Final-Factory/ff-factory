- **A disk clean-up worker starts on a low disk** (w3328, asked by Ben): a request marked `diskCleanup` for a machine (`request_work` / `update_work` `disk_cleanup: "<machine id>"`, or the dispatcher's `start_agent` `disk_cleanup: true`) starts its workers there past the low-disk gate (warn and critical), down to a 3 GB floor (`DISK_CLEANUP_FLOOR_GB`); every other new worker and editor still waits. Before this, LothDesktop's clean-up workers for w3247 and w3327 were refused by the very gate they were sent to clear (16 and 17 GB free, critical line 20 GB). The flag is never read from a title.
