- **An idle CI runner is no longer read as stuck after each job (w6001):** after a job completes, a GitHub runner's listener logs one more `status=Busy` poll, cancelled as it goes back to Online. The Linux runner-unit watcher read that line as busy, so it restarted idle listeners as "busy ... with no job worker" about 10 minutes after every job and skipped their once-only TMPDIR restart. A cancelled Busy poll, or Busy polls after a completed job with no new job handed, now count as idle.
