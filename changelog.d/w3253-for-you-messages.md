- **Your orchestrator's `[for you]` messages wait over the message box (w3253):** a message of your own orchestrator that starts with `[for you]` (something you must act on, decide or answer) shows with a For you tag in the chat and waits in the bubble over the message box, beside other people's messages, until you reply to it, your orchestrator closes it after you answer it in a later message (`close_question`), or you clear it. Its ask_person questions stay on the Waiting on input tab, linked from the bubble. Needs a portal deploy. [docs/orchestrators.md](docs/orchestrators.md), "Your orchestrator's `[for you]` messages".
