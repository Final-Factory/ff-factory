- A computer at or over its Unity editor limit has no room for new game work (w4775, lothsahn): the Capacity block shows it BUSY, "Next new game-repo work", the queue's auto-start, placement and sandbox reuse skip it, and timed work waits for it rather than going there; agent-only work and a worker taking over an editor already up in a free sandbox are unaffected. Two causes of the over-limit count are fixed too: a build's Unity whose parent chain broke is counted once, not twice, and `unity start` takes its slot one at a time, so three starts in one minute no longer all pass with two slots free.
