- **A worker waiting on an FF Factory fix is resumed when it deploys** (w2457, asked by lothsahn): a stopped worker whose last report says it waits on FF Factory or the portal ("blocked on FF Factory letting lothdesktop grant …"), with no blocked_on and no open request named, gets that wait recorded as a gate on the next portal deploy, and the deploy resumes it. w2434's worker sat idle 3.5 hours after the fix it waited on deployed. A wait on CI or a pull request in the ff-factory repo is not read as one. A request Blocked on another request whose worker sits idle 30 minutes with nothing to wake it (no wait, check-in, queued message or person asked) is flagged once in its log and to its people ("may wait forever"), as w2446 waited on w2434. The worker prompt says a wait on an FF Factory fix is `blocked_on` with `deploys: ["portal"]`.
