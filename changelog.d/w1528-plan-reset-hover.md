- **Hovering a plan in the usage sheet shows when each of its limits resets** (w1528, lothsahn: "hovering the plan in the plan usage tab causes it to show the time and day the plan resets"). The hover lists the 5-hour session, the weekly and each per-model weekly limit with its reset in your own time zone, the zone named, and the time left: "Resets Sun 11:00 MDT (in 2 d 4 h)". A limit with no reset time says "Reset time unknown" instead of a guess; one whose reset time passed says so. The hover is a card of its own (a browser's title tooltip waits a second and never shows on a phone). The formatting is `shared/resetText.ts`; tests: `server/resetText.test.ts`, `e2e/meters.spec.ts`.
