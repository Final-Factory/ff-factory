- **A worker closes a request fully covered by other work** (w3248, asked by lothsahn: "Please make sure workers close out work requests if they're fully superseded by another"): a report line `SUPERSEDED: wNNN by <what covers it>` closes a request the worker holds or its request names as cancelled, "superseded by …", and tells its people; one it does not hold or name, or one another worker is still on, goes to the dispatcher to decide. Not while a PR of it is open. The worker brief and the dispatcher's duplicate rule say to close superseded requests rather than leave them open ([docs/orchestrators.md](docs/orchestrators.md)).
