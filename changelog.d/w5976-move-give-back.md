- **A move that fails to start gives the work back to its old worker (w5976, with `movedAwayWorkers` "on"):** a request whose room or away move could not start its new session used to sit Queued with no worker; it now stays with the worker it was to leave, which resumes when a sandbox frees on its own computer, and that target computer is skipped for 30 minutes. "warn" (the default) keeps the hand-back to the dispatcher. [docs/machines.md](docs/machines.md), the w5942 section.
