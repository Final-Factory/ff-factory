- **Nothing else runs beside a timed run** (w1829, asked by lothsahn: "When the exclusive lock is held, only that worker should be running. Players, editors, and other work should shutdown to allow for proper timing"). While another worker's exclusive bench ticket holds a machine's lock, the daemon closes every other sandbox's Unity editor (out of play mode, scenes and assets saved through the editor, then quit; never killed) and asks every other worker's game player to quit (its window's close, or SIGTERM), refuses Unity MCP calls into a closed editor with why and when it comes back, and starts the editors again in the next shared turn when the ticket is released, its holder dies or it runs past its bound; a daemon restart reopens them too. The sandbox panel and `list_sandboxes` say "closed for a timed run on <machine>/<sandbox> (<request>); reopens when it ends". [docs/unity-lifecycle.md](docs/unity-lifecycle.md), "The exclusive turn".
