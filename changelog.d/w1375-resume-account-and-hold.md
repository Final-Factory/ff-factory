- **A resume now follows the Claude pool and the machine hold (w1375, w1376).** A worker whose live process is on a retired or used-up vault token restarts on its person's usable account before its next message (and waits on the pool when none is usable), and the ledger cleanup reads the person's pool, not the one account that hit its limit. A computer in `placement.hold` resumes no agent either: messages to its agents (dispatch, cleanup, CI unblock, `message_agent`, check-ins) wait in the send queue and the request reads Blocked on that machine until the hold is lifted.
