- **The portal accepts FFBox's third player report kind, hang** (w1780): a frozen game's watchdog report, id `<stamp>-hang-<suffix>`. It is handled like a crash everywhere: the provider intake feed and its signature groups, FFBox diagnoses (a `crashSignature` allowed, desync-only fields refused, ledger key `hang:<signature>`), `Report: <id>` lines and `report:<id>` keys, `ffbox_activity` and `list_ffbox_files` (`kind: hang`), the Providers panel and the connector contract.
