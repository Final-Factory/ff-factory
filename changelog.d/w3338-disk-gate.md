- **Low-disk pauses resume by themselves (w3338):** a worker that ends its turn because its machine's disk is low ("paused for low disk" in its report, or `blocked_on` with `disk: true`, which the `[disk critical]` notice now asks for) leaves its request Blocked on disk space on that machine instead of Stalled, and FF Factory resumes it once the machine's sandbox volume is 5 GB above its warning line. Disk clean-up requests are never gated on it. [docs/orchestrators.md](docs/orchestrators.md) "Waiting, Queued, Blocked".
