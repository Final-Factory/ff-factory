- **Commit-charge check, warn-only (w6411):** on Windows a machine's daemon now reads the commit charge (RAM plus page file) every 15 s and, before an editor start or a unity-slot grant, logs whether that start would fit in it (10 GB per editor, a 4 GB reserve and 8 GB per CI job running on the machine's runners): `commit check (warn-only, nothing refused): would refuse ...`. It refuses nothing yet, and a bug in it cannot change a start or a grant (each step is guarded); its lines come at most once a minute per kind of start, and a Windows machine whose read fails says so once. `add_machine commit_check off` turns it off with nobody at the machine. The RAM wait messages now show the commit figure beside RAM ("commit 123.0 of 127.8 GB"). After BEAST ran out of commit on 2026-11-27 (w6409). [docs/machines.md](docs/machines.md).
