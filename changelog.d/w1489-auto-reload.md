- **An open tab moves to the new version by itself after a deploy** (w1489, asked by lothsahn: "make ff-factory automatically reload after a deploy instead of me having to click the reload button"). The reload used to happen only for an idle tab; a tab with the message box focused (nearly every tab) or a card open got a "Reload" bar instead. Now the tab reloads once the new build answers `/api/health` and serves its own page (not while the portal is draining or starting), a few seconds apart from other tabs, and what a reload would lose is kept first: the message box (draft and caret), a "Deny and say why" note, a decline reason and an answer being written, and the chats' and lists' scroll places; an open `ask_person` question or approval comes back from the server as it was. A hand on the keys makes the tab wait until it is off them. What cannot be kept (pictures and files not yet sent, an open dialog, text in a box that keeps no draft) still makes the tab wait, with the bar as the fallback; it reloads by itself once that is gone. A reload that does not bring the new build is not repeated. Web UI only. Tests: `server/freshness.test.ts`, `e2e/freshness.spec.ts`.
