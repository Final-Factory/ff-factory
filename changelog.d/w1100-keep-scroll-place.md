- **Leaving a chat and coming back keeps your place** (w1100, asked by lothsahn): switching from the orchestrator to the Dispatcher pane (or any other page, tab or chat) and back left the chat part-way up with "New messages" showing, because the pictures in it load again, lazily, after it is drawn and the scroll handler took that growth for you scrolling up. A chat at the bottom now stays at the bottom, with what came meanwhile; one scrolled up returns to the same message (kept by message, not by pixels, so a narrower pane does not move it), with "New messages" only if something newer came in. The Dispatcher's Requests and Intake lists, the Overview board, Max and a standing agent's lists keep their scroll position too. Web UI only. [docs/ui-review.md](docs/ui-review.md).
