- **FFU work stops refilling a low drive** (w3396, asked by Ben): while a machine's sandbox drive has under 30 GB free, a new FFU (Final-Factory/ffu) request stays Queued for it ("its sandbox drive has 12.5 GB free, under the 30 GB a new FFU (cargo) worker needs") and start_agent refuses it there; it starts on a computer with room or once the drive is freed. And while free space is below the soft threshold, a running or blocked worker's Rust build folder (`ffu-target`) now goes too, once no cargo, rustc or linker runs in that worker, no process uses the folder and nothing in it was written for 15 minutes; the worker's next build makes it again (3 to 5 min clean on BEAST, measured). With ffu PR #381 (line tables only, no debug info for dependencies) one clean debug test build's target is 5.5 GB instead of 14.1 GB (measured), and ffu PR #378 cleans the CI runners' targets when their drive is under 30 GB.
