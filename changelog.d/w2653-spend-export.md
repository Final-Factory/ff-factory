- **A per-session record of model and effort, and a spend export analysts can read** (w2653, asked by Lothsahn): every session's spend record now keeps what it ran on (`runs`: model and reasoning effort, each change, each marked recorded or inferred) and its sandbox, and every live turn goes to a turn log (`data/spend-turns/`) so any time range is exact. Past sessions are backfilled from the session state and the spend data, with a default marked inferred. Workers on a request whose person's orchestrator grants `spend_read` (`request_work` or `update_work`, like `ledger_read`) get `spend_export`: CSV by request, session, model, effort and role, with the workers' and coordinators' cost apart and each request's status, filed and closed times. Read-only, no accounts, tokens or keys. `spend_report` is unchanged. [docs/spend.md](docs/spend.md), "The export".
