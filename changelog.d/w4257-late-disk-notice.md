- **A late disk notice no longer stops a worker (w4257):** `blocked_on` with `disk: true` is refused while the worker's machine is already at or above the line a disk wait clears at (its warning line + 5 GB, the same reading the gate uses), and the `[disk critical]` notice now carries its time and reading and says to carry on when that refusal comes. On 2026-11-15 biscuit's notice went out at 8 GB free and the clean-up had it at 24 GB 27 seconds later, but two workers read it minutes afterwards and stopped with 40 GB free.
