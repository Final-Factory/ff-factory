- **A worker, standing agent or orchestrator moves to a fresh Claude token once its own runs out** (w1745): agents keep their token while it works, so it is used up before its reset. When Claude refuses a token (a rejected rate-limit answer or "You've hit your limit") it counts as used up until that window resets; every agent on it moves to the best token of its person's pool at its next turn (never mid-turn), and one the limit stopped is resumed there at once in the same conversation, sandbox and requests. With no token of the pool free its requests are Blocked on the token's usage limit, as before. Never another person's pool; the dispatcher stays on its own token. Each switch is logged on the request, in the journal and in system_status (docs/vault.md section 4, "When a token runs out").
