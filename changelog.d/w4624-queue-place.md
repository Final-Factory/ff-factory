- **A timed run keeps its bench-queue place across a daemon restart (w4624):** a run waiting for the exclusive bench ticket no longer goes to the back of the queue at every deploy. The daemon keeps the waiting ticket's place in `timing/runs/<run>/queue-place.json` and the restarted daemon takes the ticket again with `bench_queue.py hold --place`. [docs/machines.md](docs/machines.md), "A waiting run keeps its bench-queue place across a daemon restart".
