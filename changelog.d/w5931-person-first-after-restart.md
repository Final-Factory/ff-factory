- **A person's message comes first after a restart, and their turn answers them** (w5931, lothsahn: "my next message to you took over a minute to respond. Why this long delay?" and "You never replied earlier to that question"). After a deploy, a launch that a person's orchestrator message waits on no longer queues behind the dispatcher's. It runs beside it, and the harness's own launches still go one at a time. A person's message to an orchestrator that is still launching opens the first turn, ahead of the harness messages already in its input. A harness message (`[dispatch]`, `[follow-ups to file]`, `[ops worker]`) sent during a person's orchestrator turn now waits for that turn to end and opens a turn of its own; only a relay about that person (the rest of their FFBox turn) and their own reaction join it. Before this, the 05:35Z deploy's turn put its only text into answering a `[dispatch]`, and the person's four questions were answered only in its thinking. The re-hash after the tool refresh now starts at the first rewritten byte instead of reading the whole 1-2.5 GB transcript again. Each launch logs a `launch timing:` line with the time of every step. Docs: docs/restart.md "The first answer after a restart (w5931)", docs/transcript-seal.md. Tests: server/personFirst.test.ts, server/transcriptSeal.test.ts.
