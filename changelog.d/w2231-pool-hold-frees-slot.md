- **A worker whose Claude pool is full is Blocked on it and frees its sandbox** (w2231, asked by lothsahn): when a worker's next message cannot start its process because its person's pool is held (a usage limit, the dispatcher's reserve, the one-job cap), its requests show Blocked on that pool's usage limit with the reset time instead of Queued, its sandbox is released for other work (branch kept, unsaved work saved first), and it is resumed and placed again when the pool has room. A worker mid-turn is never stopped; a pair run's leg keeps its place. Details: [docs/orchestrators.md](docs/orchestrators.md), "A worker whose Claude pool is held".
