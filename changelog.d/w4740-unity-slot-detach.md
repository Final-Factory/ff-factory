- `unity-slot run --detach` and `unity-slot wait <id>` (w4740, r290): a build whose queue for a Unity slot may be long waits and runs in a process of its own, so a background task killed at Claude Code's 2 h limit no longer takes a queued build with it (w4488's build on LothDesktop queued 1 h 50 min and was killed 10 min in). `wait` follows its log, exits with its code, and can be run again.
