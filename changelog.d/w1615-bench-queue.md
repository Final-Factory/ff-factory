- **The bench lock is a first-come, first-served queue that covers builds and editor boots** (w1615, asked by lothsahn): `unity-slot run` takes a shared ticket in the machine's bench queue after its Unity slots (`--bench exclusive` for a timed run), and waits at most 2 minutes for a slot when its caller already holds the bench lock; a sandbox `unity start` takes one after its slot check and, while a timed run holds the lock or waits ahead, answers `Queued (w1615)` and starts the editor itself at its turn, letting the ticket go once the MCP bridge is up; the daemon counts a queue holder while its process lives, with no 40-minute age rule. The queue itself is the game repo's `scripts/bench/bench_queue.py` (`Documentation/Bench-Lock.md` there); [docs/unity-lifecycle.md](docs/unity-lifecycle.md), "The bench queue".
