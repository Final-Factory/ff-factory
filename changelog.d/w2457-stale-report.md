- **A worker's old "still open" report no longer makes a new wait after it was woken** (w2457, asked by lothsahn): the ledger reads a stopped worker's report as a wait only when the report is newer than the moment the request's last wait ended. On the first live deploy of the w2457 change, w2457's own "blocked on a portal deploy" line, written before that deploy woke it, was read a minute later, while the worker's resume was still being placed, and became a wait on the next deploy.
