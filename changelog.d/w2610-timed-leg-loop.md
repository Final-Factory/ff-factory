- **A timed leg's own worker no longer files a leg of the leg** (w2610, asked by Ben): a w2544 drop of a timed leg's wait now widens it, so `timed_place` answers "here" for its worker on any computer that takes timed runs (the rule the room flag already used); otherwise it answers "not here" and the dispatcher is told to move it, with no new request. A block on a request merged into the blocked request itself is refused or cleared at the merge, and a parent waiting on its timed leg's report is no longer woken when the leg is Blocked on anything but the parent itself.
