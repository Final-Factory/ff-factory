- **A Live view kept open says why** (w1746 follow-up): the portal journal logs `live idle: <machine>/<sandbox>: ... stays
  open: <reason>` (a page watching, a turn, a check-in, idle N of 15 min) when the reason changes, and system_status's
  "Live view idle close" line lists each view kept open with its reason, so a view that looks idle and stays open can be
  explained from the logs.
