- **The ops worker takes follow-ups from harness turns for 1 hour, not 12** (w5508, lothsahn: "Shorten ops workers follow up to 1 hour only. Regular workers don't shorten"; finding 8 of the w5452 audit). A check-in, a timer or a relayed `[ops worker]` report can follow up a job only in its first hour; after that the send is refused and needs the person's own turn (a new job, or `follow_up: true`). The person's own follow-up and regular workers are unchanged.
